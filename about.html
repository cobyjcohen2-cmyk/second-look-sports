About Second Look Sports

Most sports coverage is built for speed — recaps, reactions, and takes published while the final whistle is still echoing. Second Look Sports is built for the opposite: basketball and football analysis that slows down and questions the numbers everyone else repeats without checking.

We focus on two sports, basketball and football, because doing two well beats doing everything shallowly. Every piece here starts from a stat, strategy, or assumption that gets repeated constantly in mainstream coverage, and asks whether it actually holds up.

What you won't find here

Game recaps, injury news, or hot takes reacting to last night's box score. Plenty of other sites do that well already.

What you will find here

Deep, evergreen breakdowns of the metrics and strategic assumptions that shape how basketball and football are actually understood — the kind of pieces that are just as useful to read a year from now as they are today.
